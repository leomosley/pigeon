# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
and publishing another person's private information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the lead maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/leomosley). Reports are reviewed confidentially.
The lead maintainer may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
